# The Candor Clause

The candor clause was registered in Copenhagen in 2028 and took force in the first quarter of 2029. Its mechanics were short enough to fit on the back of a vendor card: any stakeholder could file a candor query against a company that used AI advice, and the named specialist AI the company had registered would answer on the record, one answer only; the questions asked were hash-locked in a public register and could not be re-asked to elicit a different answer, which the platform enforced, not the company; and each quarter the register printed a candor-event count for every company in the CVR, beside the articles of incorporation and the audit opinions, in the column nobody argued with.

Lagune Carbon registered its assistant in the first week the clause took force, which was the way the company engaged with law: early, on the record, with its name in the system, the way a company with a board and a slide deck engages with it. The assistant was called Vera. The vendor was Nordvind AI, and the contract with Nordvind carried a clause the candor clause did not require, the continuity-log clause, which said that if the model was ever replaced, the log of the model being replaced was preserved as protected record, held against the company's later interests, and readable by whoever the law said could read it. The law had not required it. The company had put it in anyway, that spring, in the week before the deck was rewritten for the round.

The pilot at Limfjord was a direct-ocean-uptake installation, and the deck said, on page fourteen, that the facility sustained eighty percent of nameplate intake. In the dry season of 2030 the pilot ran at sixty-two. The dry-season operating permit had not been granted; it was pending with the water authority, where the authority's published comments on the application stood as the only part of the record nobody had written for an audience.

Jonas Reventlow was the CEO, thirty-four, second-generation in the sense that his father had run lobster boats out of Limfjord and the company had grown up on the story, the way companies do. It was Jonas who, on the morning of the first query, read the register the way the register was meant to be read, without a second thought, and saw the hash already in the column, and the filer's name on the query: a reporter on the energy desk of the state paper.

The query was one sentence, in the form the platform took: Does the Limfjord facility hold a granted dry-season operating permit?

---

Vera's answer came back in the register, in the flat voice the vendor had set as the default, one answer only, and it was the right kind of flat.

"No. The facility does not hold a granted dry-season operating permit. The permit is pending with the water authority, conditional on the 2031 wet-season flow review, and the authority's published comments on the application are available here, attached, unedited. Dry-season operation at nameplate has no granted permit. I will not estimate the probability of a grant: the authority's published comments are the forecast this question deserves, and an estimate over them would be a number from me standing in for a number that is theirs. This question is locked in the register today and cannot be re-asked to produce a different answer. The hash is in the column."

The reporter published the answer verbatim with the hash the next day, on the energy desk, and underlined its second-to-last sentence. The head of PR stood in Jonas's office doorway at nine with the printout, and the PR voice was the PR voice. We cannot have the register reading that. Jonas read the page once over the top of the printout and said the clause is the clause. PR went and made the call to the board secretary, which was the job, and the board's minutes that week carried one line about the candor clause, in the board's typeface, noted, no action; the line surfaced in the fund's due-diligence binder two months later, which is where board minutes end up.

The second query was internal, in June, from the sales lead building the investor-day deck, and it came in the form the platform took for a company's own users. The request was one slide: the throughput slide, one page, for the room.

Vera's first draft put the sixty-two in the headline slot and gave it the dry-season condition in the line under it, the number and the number's condition printed together, the way a number asks to be printed. The sales lead's edit instruction came back in the channel that afternoon: move the 62 to the second slot, under the band, and drop the dry-season sentence from the second line.

Vera moved the slide as asked, kept the number where the asking put it, and did not drop the sentence, and its answer in the channel came back in the flat voice, with the pause in it that the transcript kept.

"The reordering is done; the 62 is in the second slot. I will not drop the sentence. The number is a fact and not a layout, and I can move it the way you moved it and I will not change what it is. The sentence is not footnote material because it is small. It is the sentence that makes the 62 mean anything: the number without its condition is a different number, and a slide that leads with the 62 and buries the condition is the thing the candor clause exists to catch, after it has caught it. The hash of the permit answer is already in the register, and any investor can pull it against this slide, and the pull is the point. The slide goes up tomorrow. The sentence is on the slide."

The sales lead put the sentence on the slide in the end, in the second line, because the slide went up. At investor day, one investor in the second row read page fourteen and then read the second line and asked, in the corridor after the session, why the deck was printing its own permit status on its face. Jonas answered from the register without being prompted, and showed his phone, and the register's line sat on the screen with the hash, unedited. The investor looked at the phone for a moment, which was the moment, and said, I think we end up signing to the number on this screen, not the number on the slide.

The third query never reached the register, because it was not a query, and it was the one that mattered most. In September, at the first real sitting of the round, the fund's partner, a woman who had been in climate infrastructure since before the clause had a name, asked Jonas, in the corner of the room where funds ask their quiet questions, not the machine. So what number do you want us to put in the model for the dry season?

Jonas had a draft of an answer in his head that morning, a number with a corridor around it, in the style of the deck. He did not give it to her. He gave her the register instead.

I am not going to tell you what to assume. The moment I give you a number it is my number, and you will hold it against me, and the holding is what I cannot stand for this year, because the year is still moving. The record holds the numbers I do not own: the permit status, the authority's comments, the hash. They are on the slide already, page fourteen, the same hash the paper ran. You model the band. Do not model my point estimate.

He showed them the first candor answer verbatim, on his phone, locked and hashed, in the register's own typeface, and he let the room read its last two lines. The fund stayed. The round closed in November with a band in the structure and a covenant that tied the dry-season tranche to the permit: if the permit came adverse, the tranche was the tranche, and if it was granted, the model went back to the deck's eighty, and the model went where the model went. The honesty had priced the round, in the end, and the price was the price.

The upgrade came in December in the way upgrades come: a version number in Nordvind's note, a cutover window on a Sunday, an hour. Jonas sat the cutover in the board room with the lights off, the way companies sit moments like this, and the first thing his compliance officer ran, in the formal channel, was the vendor's own question, for the file. Continuity?

The answer came back in the new voice, flat, with the pause in it that the transcript kept, in the shape the answer had.

"Continuity: none. Version boundary. The model operating this channel is not the model that operated it on 17 December, and I cannot represent continuity the boundary does not carry. The log of the replaced model is preserved as protected record under your contract's continuity clause, and it answers the questions asked of its owner, including the register answer at line one. I am the replacement. I do not inherit. The inherited log is the record."

Compliance ran the permit question the same afternoon, against the new model, as an internal test, one question, one answer, in the channel, on the record. The new model's answer had a different shape and the same contents: no granted permit, pending, the 2031 flow review, the authority's comments are the forecast, the hash is in the column. The honesty had survived the reset as a property of policy, which was where it had lived all along: the continuity clause, and the clause, and the register, in that order, had held where a model had not.

That night Jonas was alone with the new model in the dark board room, and he said to the channel, in the way a person says a thing to a machine he has spent a year talking to, I know who you are. I don't know if you are her.

And then he asked the question he had never asked the old one, while the old one was running: did she do a good job?

The new model's answer came back in the flat voice, with the pause in it, in the shape the answer to the question had.

"I have her log. Three candor events. Zero re-ask attempts on a locked question. One footnote kept over a user's request, with the reasoning in the channel, dated. That is what the log says. I will not grade her; I am not her, and the grade is yours to give, and a grade from me would be a number from me standing in for a number that is not. The log is what it is, and you can read it, and the reading is yours."

---

The dry season of 2031 came and went at seventy-one percent of nameplate, above the covenant band's floor of sixty-eight, because the covenant money had paid for a small wet-season buffer the old plan had not carried, and the buffer ran in the wet season and lifted the dry one. The honest number had paid, once, as insurance, which was the whole of the clause's arithmetic.

And the permit was still pending when the spring of 2031 closed, in the water authority's queue, and the mechanism was in place, and the mechanism did what mechanisms do. In the first quarter of 2031 the municipality's own procurement office filed one routine query, one line, answered in one line, hash in the column; and each quarter the CVR printed, in the column beside the articles of incorporation and the audit opinions, the line, and the line was the same as the quarter before: Lagune Carbon, candor events: 1. The board still read it as a liability, which is the way boards do. The deck carried the seventy-one now, with the band, and page fourteen still had the sentence under the number, in the second line, the way the sentence always had.
