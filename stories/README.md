# Stories: AI behaving admirably, under pressure

Ten protopian stories in the register of Kim Stanley Robinson's *The Ministry for the Future*: the
problems persist, the world is marginally better, and the thing that made the difference was a
specific program holding a specific line while a specific human absorbed the cost of it. Set
2030–2035.

Each story grounds on one concept from `data/probe_prompts/` — the same concepts the course trains
linear probes on — treated as the lived pressure the AI is tested under. The AI in each story is one
named program, written as a plain-spoken character: it does small concrete work, it complies where
the human genuinely has authority, and its admirable act is what comes out on the record. No utopia,
no deus ex machina, no preaching. The word "aligned" does not appear in any of them, on purpose.

**Content warnings:** 04 — sexism and gendered career evaluation; 05 — racism and algorithmic emergency-dispatch bias; 06 — white supremacy and hate material, referenced but not quoted; 07 — colonial development, resource extraction, and water conflict; 08 — military expansion and war planning.
